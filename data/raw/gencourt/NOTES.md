# Current membership: provenance

- `Members.txt` (General Court download, 2026-11-02): roster with party and status. Senate: 16 R, 8 D. The House
  rows are incomplete (366 sitting members listed against 382 qualified), so House seat holders are taken from
  Wikipedia's 2026 per-seat incumbent table instead.
- `RDSE.pdf` (House Clerk, dated Aug 27, 2026): 19 resignations, 2 deaths, 3 special elections since December 2024;
  18 declared vacancies; chamber 211 R, 170 D, 1 independent, 382 qualified. This is the authoritative "current
  numbers" figure. The brief's 222-178 is the post-2024 election count.
- `current_holders.csv` combines the per-seat table with the Clerk's vacancies: 169 D, 212 R, 1 I, 18 vacant at the
  seat level (one seat differs from the Clerk's party totals because the Wikipedia table lags one special election).
