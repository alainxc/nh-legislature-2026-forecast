## Quirks
- Event-marker rows (colspan notes such as primaries, conventions, candidate withdrawals) are skipped; counts are therefore lower than raw table row counts (e.g. CD2 2026 table: 9 polls + 1 event row; Harris-Trump 2024 table: 13 poll rows + 7 event rows, 2 Emerson/CES polls have two rows each).
- Multi-row polls (rowspan: alternate samples/leaner variants) produce one CSV row each; later ones are flagged 'additional row for same poll' in notes. Some rows have no sample size (source shows only '(LV)').
- Wikipedia's 2024 Harris-Trump table contains a Trafalgar Group row dated 2021-12-10..12 (identical to the 2022 Senate Trafalgar poll; likely a Wikipedia mislabel). Kept as in source; downweight/drop by date.
- The 2026 NH CD1/CD2 tables include 2025 polls (cycle 2026); only the national generic ballot is cut at 2026-01-01.
- Header 'Lead' column of the national table is ignored; margin_dem is recomputed.
