The 2026 New Hampshire House of Representatives election will be held on November 3, 2026, alongside the other 2026 United States elections.[1] Voters will elect all 400 members of the New Hampshire House of Representatives to serve a two-year term.[2]

Background

[edit]

In the 2024 New Hampshire House of Representatives election, Republicans picked up 21 seats, expanding their narrow one-seat majority.[3]
