The 2024 New Hampshire Senate elections were held on November 5, 2024, to elect all 24 seats in the New Hampshire Senate. Primary elections were held on September 10, 2024.[1] The elections coincided with the Presidential, U.S. House, and State House elections.

Republicans expanded on their 14-10 majority and earned a 16-8 supermajority after the elections.[2]

Retirements

[edit]

Four incumbents did not seek re-election.

Democrats

[edit]
  • District 15: Becky Whitley is retiring.[3]
  • District 20: Lou D'Allesandro is retiring.[4]

Republicans

[edit]
  • District 1: Carrie Gendreau is retiring.[5]
  • District 3: Jeb Bradley is retiring.[6]
