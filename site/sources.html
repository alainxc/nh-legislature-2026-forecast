Data audit: what exists and what is missing

Data audit: what exists for a New Hampshire Legislature 2026 forecast (as of 2026-11-02)

Checked every item in the brief against the live source. Verdicts: OK (official or official-derived, usable as is),

PARTIAL (usable with a documented workaround), GAP (not available from here; the site says so).

1. Election returns by town and ward, 2020-2024

ItemVerdictFinding
NH Secretary of State results pagesGAP (direct)sos.nh.gov returns HTTP 403 to every non-browser request and the in-app browser is blocked from the domain by organisation policy. No Wayback snapshot exists for the 2024 results page or any 2026 general-election candidate page (only the 2026 primary pages and the 2026-election-details stub were archived).
OpenElections openelections-data-nhOKTown/ward-level CSVs transcribed from the SoS tabulations for every 2022 and 2024 general-election race (president, governor, US Senate, US House, Executive Council, State Senate, State Representative) plus one combined 2020 general file (president, governor, US Senate, US House, State Senate, State House). Statewide sums match the certified totals to within a few votes (2024 president D 418,488 / R 395,523 vs certified 418,496 / 395,523; 2024 governor 360,149 / 436,122; 2022 governor 256,766 / 352,813; 2022 US Senate 332,490 / 275,631). Cities are reported by ward (Manchester 12, Nashua 9, Concord 10, Dover 6, Rochester 6, Keene 5, Laconia 6, Lebanon 3, Portsmouth 5, Somersworth 5, Franklin 3, Claremont 3, Berlin 4).
OpenElections quirks foundPARTIAL(a) The 2020 file carries county-subtotal and TOTALS rows that must be dropped (they double the statewide sum). (b) Unincorporated places are spelled differently across years (Atk. & Gilm. Ac. Gt. vs At. & Gil. Academy Grant vs Atkinson & Gilmanton Academy Grant); a name-normalisation table is needed. (c) State Representative files label Dover Ward 3 as Strafford 15 in both years; by statute Dover Ward 2 is Strafford 15 and Dover Ward 3 is Strafford 16, so the row is relabelled. In 2022 the Dover Ward 3 Democratic candidate's votes appear under Scatter (1,766 votes). (d) 2022 state-rep rows carry surnames only; 2024 rows carry full names. (e) Party codes include suffix noise (JR., R); last token is the party. (f) Floterial districts are suffixed F (e.g. Belknap 8F). With the Dover fix, the aggregated 2024 seat split reproduces the official 222 R / 178 D and 2022 reproduces 201 R / 199 D to within one or two seats before recount adjustments.
MIT Election Data + Science Labnot neededPrecinct returns exist for 2020 and 2022 but add nothing beyond OpenElections for NH (towns are the precincts); kept as a cross-check reference only.

Consequences for the model

1. Town-level baselines are exact for every district (towns and wards are the atoms of NH districts), including floterials, for 2024 president, 2024 governor, 2022 governor, 2022 US Senate and 2020 president.

2. Candidate data rests on Ballotpedia's transcription of SoS filings rather than the filings themselves; the site discloses this on every page that uses it.

3. There is one direct legislative generic-ballot poll, so the statewide environment leans on the governor race (with a large personal-vote correction for Ayotte, who ran 12 points ahead of the 2024 presidential result), the two congressional districts, the statewide congressional preference and the national generic ballot.

4. House 2022 incumbency for the backtest comes from Wikipedia's per-district incumbent column, since 2020 results are on the old map.
